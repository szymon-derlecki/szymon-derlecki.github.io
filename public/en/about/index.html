About

Since an early age, I have been driven by the desire to become the best version of myself and create a real impact. Throughout the last two years in Denmark, I have developed my skills under the guidance of mentors collaborating closely with organisations such as the Danish Armed Forces, some of whom have also been recognised by structures such as NATO. Technology has always been my natural environment.

Although it may sound unusual, my journey started with construction and agricultural machinery, evolved through carpentry - a skill I still practice and greatly value - and eventually led me to contributing to the development of advanced technologies with applications in areas such as defence, intelligence, and search-and-rescue operations.

Looking back, I have consistently chosen the most challenging problems because they offered the greatest opportunities to learn, grow, and create solutions with real-world value.

Life has also thrown challenges at me. Barely three weeks after moving abroad for my master’s studies, a serious injury turned my carefully planned path upside down. Instead of giving up, I reflected on the situation, drew conclusions, managed to meet the right people whose support helped me significantly, and gradually started implementing my own solutions again - combining my academic journey with my passions.

When I attended a Martin Garrix concert in Lithuania and someone accidentally ruined a recording of one of his rarely performed mashups, I decided to take matters into my own hands. I brought back six years of experience in music transcription, and my passion for audio engineering accelerated like Verstappen in Austria 2019 - a disastrous start, quickly finding the motivation to fight back, and a victory claimed at the very last moment.

My personality is rooted in continuous self-development and a strong commitment to delivering results, regardless of the challenges I encounter. I understand the importance of effective communication - both with the people I work with and those I meet throughout my journey. In every collaboration, I bring full commitment and do my best to ensure that it leads to the best possible outcomes. This approach has been recognised through recommendations from people I have had the opportunity to work with.
